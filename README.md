# 🏡 Build a Life

A fun, colourful, friendly life simulation game for children aged 5–15.
Start as a baby and live a whole life — growing up, going to school, finding a job,
making friends, adopting pets, falling in love, raising a family and enjoying old age.

> **No real money.** All coins are pretend. There are no purchases, no adverts, no chat
> and no accounts. Games are saved only in the browser on this device.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (double-click works — no install or server needed),
or run `npm start` and visit `http://localhost:8080/`.

The site is published to GitHub Pages automatically when `main` changes (see `.github/workflows/pages.yml`;
enable it once under Settings → Pages → Source: "GitHub Actions").

1. **Make a player** — each person who plays gets their own profile.
2. **Create a character** in 5 easy steps — name & identity, face & hair, clothes, accessories, family & home town.
   Each player can save up to 12 characters, and **every character has their own saved life**.
3. **Complete your 3 challenges** 🎯 each year — they unlock your next birthday! Tap **Go** to jump to the right place, or **Play** for a mini-game. Swap one you don't fancy with 🔄 (once a year).
   Use your ⚡ energy on activities, studying, work, friends and pets; if it runs out before the challenges are done, **Rest & recharge**.
4. **Tick off your daily jobs** ✅ for a bonus.
5. **Tap 🪞 Change Your Look** whenever you like — it's always free.
6. **Press 🎂 Age Up** to have a birthday — coins come in, bills go out, and life events happen.
7. When the story ends in old age, **play as your child** or **start a brand new life**.

## What's in the game

| Area | Features |
|---|---|
| 👥 Players & characters | Multiple player profiles; each player saves up to 12 characters, each with their own life, plus a Hall of Fame of finished lives |
| ✨ Character creation | Name, identity (girl, boy, non-binary or "just me") and pronouns, 6 skin tones, 5 eye colours, 9 hairstyles, 10 hair colours, full outfits, 11 hats/glasses/hair accessories, 11 extras, 6 family types, 7 home towns |
| 🪞 Change Your Look | Free at any time from the character card or the Shop: change name, identity, pronouns, face, hair, clothes and accessories, and plan outfits for future ages |
| 👕 Clothing collections | A separate collection and saved outfit for **Babies, Toddlers, Children, Teenagers, Adults and Older Adults** — 19 tops, 21 trousers/shorts/skirts/dresses/all-in-ones and 17 shoe styles, each in 14 colours. Every outfit is free; only 3 special items (crown, cape, golden trainers) are unlocked with pretend coins in the Shop. All clothes are available to every character |
| 🗺️ Places to live | 9 places to choose from when you create a character — towns, cities, villages, seaside towns and islands (filter by type) — each with different pay, prices, a yearly bonus and special places. Grown-ups can move |
| 🧭 Explore | An interactive map of your town. Travel between places by walking, scooter, bike, bus, car or taxi (your character moves across the map). Every town has a home, school, hospital, doctors' surgery, high-street shops, supermarket, park, café, restaurant, pet shelter, workplace, hair salon, library and a station or ferry port — plus beaches, lighthouses, museums, a cinema, a farm or ski slopes depending on where you live |
| 💬 People & objects | Over 60 things to tap (swings, duck ponds, sandcastles, fish tanks, spinny chairs…) and named townspeople to chat to — doctors, teachers, shopkeepers, baristas, hairdressers, the ferry captain and more. Some can become your friends, and your own family and friends turn up at home, school, the park and the café. Each place also has buttons for the real game actions (check-ups, groceries, adopting pets, working…) |
| 🚆 Day trips | Take the train or ferry to any other town or island for the day, and you're home again for your next birthday |
| 🎂 Growing up | Year-by-year ageing through **8 life stages** — Baby (0–2), Toddler (3–5), Child (6–12), Teenager (13–17), Young Adult (18–29), Adult (30–49), Older Adult (50–69) and Grandparent (70+) — with a welcome screen for each new stage, a life-journey strip, birthday cake & presents, milestones and 4 stats (Happiness, Health, Smarts, Fitness) |
| 🎯 Challenges | 3 challenges every year, chosen from 70 stage-specific challenges (e.g. baby play & staying happy and healthy; homework, spelling tests & making friends; school projects, presentations, exams & graduating; finding a job, learning skills & managing money; running a household, looking after children & paying bills; hobbies, retirement & family time; storytelling with grandchildren). Finishing all three unlocks the next birthday. Rewards: coins, ⭐ challenge stars, a bonus star for a full set, and 4 new stickers. "Big moment" milestones at 16–17 (exams, graduation). Challenges that become impossible are swapped automatically, and energy can always be recharged, so nobody gets stuck |
| 🎮 Mini-games | Quizzes (shapes, colours & animal sounds, counting, maths homework, spelling, presentations, exams, budgeting, household bills, crosswords) and find-it games (toys, tidying up, recycling, healthy food shopping, lost glasses). Every question can be read aloud |
| 🏫 School | Nursery, primary and secondary school, school reports, 8 after-school clubs, part-time jobs for teens, then college, university or a gap year |
| 💼 Careers | 31 full-time jobs (shop assistant, chef, hairdresser, builder, police officer, teacher, nurse, vet, doctor, astronaut and more) with education, stat and **skill** requirements; promotions and job titles; on-the-spot bonuses and a payday bonus for great work; small pay deductions for (funny) mistakes; warnings and getting fired if your work bar stays too low; retirement with a pension |
| 🧺 More than one job | Up to 3 jobs at once: a full-time job, your own business and side jobs (paper round, babysitting, tutoring, delivery rider, market stall, lifeguard…) paid per shift. Teens can have 2 part-time jobs. Every job uses a little of your yearly energy |
| 🏪 Business owner | 7 businesses (bakery, café, toy shop, flower shop, hair salon, pet grooming, app company) that grow from a market stall to a chain of shops; profit depends on effort and skills; sell it any time |
| 🛠️ Skills | 9 skills (cooking, caring, people, numbers, science, building, creativity, technology, business) learned at the free library, in paid classes, on the job and through activities. Higher skills unlock jobs, raise pay and mean fewer mistakes |
| 🪙 Money | A Money tab shows coins, money owed and exactly what comes in and goes out on your next birthday. Income: pocket money, chores, gifts, salaries, bonuses, business profit, shifts, odd jobs, pensions. Bills: groceries (choose a yearly basket — or a budget one is picked for you), energy & water, rent or home payments, repairs, cars, pets and children |
| 🛟 Safety net | Coins never go below zero. If you can't pay your bills the shortfall becomes money owed (no interest, capped — a charity covers anything above the cap), you get a Money Rescue Plan and a money-rescue challenge. There are always ways to earn: odd jobs from age 8, side jobs, jobseeker support, selling things at the car boot sale, asking family for help once a year, and moving somewhere cheaper |
| 🏠 Homes & cars | Rent or buy 10 homes (including a castle), sell or pay off your home, pass a driving test and buy 7 vehicles |
| ❤️ Relationships | Family, siblings and friends (including townspeople you befriend). Grown-ups only: a love story in steps — going on dates → boyfriend or girlfriend → moving in together (they help with the bills) → engaged → married — and buying a home together (your partner pays up to half the deposit). Babies arrive once you live together or are married; you can also adopt |
| 🧸 Raising children | Children grow up year by year with their own happiness, health and smarts. Feed them (there's always a free meal), help with homework (a quiz at their level), buy new clothes when they outgrow them (pick an outfit), celebrate their birthdays (a homemade cake is free), take them to the doctor (free), play together (activities that suit their age) and give pocket money. At 18 they grow up and choose a career. Missed things only lead to gentle notes — nobody is ever harmed |
| 🎉 Family occasions | Children's birthdays, wedding anniversaries, family days out, a winter celebration, grandchildren's visits — and for children, making cards for family birthdays. Every occasion has a free way to celebrate |
| 💬 Conversations | Tap Talk on anyone in your family for a two-way chat — they say something and you choose your reply (worries, jokes, puppies, broccoli…), with conversations that suit parents, siblings, friends, partners, toddlers, children, teenagers, grown-up children and grandchildren |
| 🐾 Pets | An animal shelter in every town with new animals each year — dogs, cats, hamsters, rabbits, guinea pigs, mice, chinchillas and other pets (fish, birds, lizards, tortoises, ponies) — each with a name, breed, age and personality. Adopt with coins (children can also ask their family once a year), choose the name and the look (fur colour, markings, eyes, accessory), then feed (everyday food is free), wash, play, pat, buy toys and beds in the pet shop, and visit the vet (free for children; there's a Vet's Surgery in every town). Pets have happiness, health, tummy and cleanliness levels, are drawn as cute pictures that show their mood, and are never harmed by a missed meal |
| 🛍️ Shopping | 9 departments — groceries, everyday essentials, toys, books, clothes, gadgets, furniture, decorations and birthday parties — over 70 items. Furniture and decorations make your home cosy (extra happiness every birthday); party items make your next birthday a party |
| 🏥 Healthcare | Doctor, dentist, flu jab, eye test (glasses!), hospital and spa; illnesses that need the right treatment; free for children |
| ☀️ My Day (everyday life) | Each year has four days (spring, summer, autumn, winter), each split into Morning, Daytime, Evening and Night. Tasks change with age: milk, bath and lullabies for babies; brushing teeth (tap the germs), washing or showers (scrub the mud), breakfast and dinner (build a plate — balanced meals are best), school or work (choose what to do), tidying or cleaning (tap the mess), homework, shopping, the doctor when poorly, looking for work, hobbies when retired, and for grown-ups paying bills early and a friendly tax return. Parents get the children ready, read bedtime stories and have family time; pet owners feed the pets. Do every ⭐ task then sleep for a **Super Day** (stars, happiness and health). New needs — energy, hygiene and tummy — go down as you're busy, with gentle reminders. Taxes (income tax above a tax-free amount, and council tax) only apply to grown-ups, and their purpose is explained. Yearly household jobs still appear below |
| 🎉 Celebrations | Birthdays, school starts, graduations, weddings, new babies, promotions, retirement — with confetti and sounds |
| 🎂 Birthdays & parties | Everyone has a birthday date. In the Celebrate tab, plan your party: theme (unicorns, dinosaurs, space, pirates… up to garden BBQs and afternoon tea), cake (flavour, shape — homemade, number, two tiers or castle — and topping), decorations, balloon colour, venue, guests and an invitation design; send invitations and get replies. At Age Up: a growing-up animation (younger you → older you), a drawn cake with real candles to blow out, a party scene with your guests and presents to unwrap (they go into My Stuff). Families always celebrate even without a plan, and children's parties are part-paid by their family. Plan your children's parties (theme, cake, party size, your present) and celebrate family & friends' birthdays in their season with cards, songs, presents or surprise parties. A free party is always possible |
| 🗓️ Seasonal celebrations | Optional — choose which ones your family celebrates: Easter, Eid al-Fitr, Summer Fair, Halloween, Bonfire Night, Diwali, Christmas, Hanukkah and Lunar New Year. Each happens in its season in My Day, with activities like egg hunts, costumes and trick-or-treating, pumpkin carving, lighting diyas or the menorah, decorating the tree, red envelopes and presents to unwrap |
| 📖 My Story | 30 collectible stickers, a life timeline and an end-of-life story with highlights and life stars |

Sad moments are handled gently: pets "cross the Rainbow Bridge", and older relatives "lived a long and
happy life". The player's own story only ends in old age.

### Look & feel

* Hand-drawn SVG artwork everywhere (no image files, so it's sharp on any screen): an animated scenic background that matches where you live (town, city, village, seaside, island, snowy mountains), illustrated buildings for every place on the town map, an outside picture of every home, and a room interior that shows the furniture and decorations you've bought, the people you live with and your pets.
* Characters with eyebrows, soft outlines and shaded clothes; cute drawn pets; drawn birthday cakes.
* Glassy rounded cards, chunky 3D buttons, colour-coded section tabs, smooth slide-in and hover animations — all calmed down automatically for players who prefer reduced motion.
* Layouts for phones, tablets and computers.

### Child-friendly design

* Big, chunky buttons, large emoji and short sentences.
* 🔊 **Read aloud** button on every pop-up (uses the browser's speech voice) for younger readers.
* Sound effects (can be muted), animations, confetti — and all motion is reduced if the device asks for reduced motion.
* Works on phones, tablets and computers; keyboard friendly.

## Code

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no build step, no dependencies (the Google Fonts are optional; the game falls back to system fonts offline).

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `js/data.js` | All game content: towns, jobs, homes, cars, pets, shop items, activities, events, stickers |
| `js/wardrobe.js` | Identities, pronouns, the six age-group clothing collections, colours and accessories |
| `js/game.js` | Game engine (pure logic, no DOM). Every action goes through `BAL.Game.run(life, action, …)` |
| `js/economy.js` | Skills, side jobs, odd jobs, businesses, bonuses & mistakes, groceries & shop departments, money owed and the rescue plan |
| `js/world.js` | Town types and islands, the place catalogue, generated town maps, townspeople, interactive objects, travel and day trips |
| `js/family.js` | Relationship steps, buying a home together, raising children, family occasions, conversations and parenting challenges |
| `js/pets.js` | The animal shelter, drawn pets and their looks, feeding, washing, toys, beds, the vet and pet care over the years |
| `js/everyday.js` | My Day: the daily routine, hygiene & tummy needs, meals, school and work moments, Super Days, paying bills early and taxes |
| `js/celebrations.js` | Birthday dates, party planning, invitations, presents, children's parties, family birthdays, seasonal festivals and the drawn birthday cake |
| `js/challenges.js` | Life-stage challenges: catalogue, yearly selection, progress tracking, rewards, the birthday lock and the mini-game question generators |
| `js/art.js` | Scenic backgrounds, house exteriors, room interiors and town buildings |
| `js/avatar.js` | SVG avatars for every life stage: full-body (clothes, trousers, skirts, dresses, shoes) and head-and-shoulders portraits |
| `js/storage.js` | Player profiles and their characters in `localStorage` (older saves are upgraded automatically), plus the tiny sound synthesiser |
| `css/theme.css` | The visual design layer: colours, cards, tabs, buttons, motion and responsive layouts |
| `js/ui.js` | Screens, panels, pop-ups, toasts and confetti |

A browser test (`node tests/ui/crawl.cjs`, needs Playwright) creates characters at every life stage and clicks every button in every tab on computer, tablet and phone screen sizes, reporting errors, buttons with no handler and anything that makes the page wider than the screen.

Engine tests live in `tests/game.test.js` and run with `npm test` (Node.js 20+, no dependencies).
